League Rules

Fixtures & Scheduling
  • Fixtures are generated by the admin and posted in the Discord server.
  • All matches must be played within the fixture week unless postponed.
  • Scheduled dates are in SAST (GMT+2). Check the calendar for public holidays.
  • Do not play on Mettlestate event days unless fixtures are confirmed.
Result Submission
  • Results must be submitted with a screenshot of the final scoreline.
  • Tag @Tyron or @Astral in the Discord with your result screenshot.
  • Both players must agree on the scoreline before it is logged.
  • Disputed results will be reviewed by the admin — decision is final.
Postponements
  • Each player has 20 postponement tokens for the season.
  • Postponing a match costs 1 token. Tokens cannot be transferred.
  • Attempting to postpone with 0 tokens results in an automatic 3–0 forfeit.
  • To postpone, tag @Tyron or @Astral in Discord and mention the fixture.
Prohibited Conduct
  • Unsportsmanlike behaviour, harassment, or trash talk will not be tolerated.
  • Using exploits, glitches, or modded accounts is grounds for immediate removal.
  • Colluding to manipulate standings is a permanent ban offence.
